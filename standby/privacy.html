StandBy

Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android app published on Google Play as StandBy (com.standby.app).

Last updated: October 10, 2026 (ads and subscription sections added)  ·  Türkçe

In short

StandBy has no accounts and no analytics or tracking, and never sends your personal data to a server of ours. The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads only on the Settings screen — never on the clock screen or the lock screen. An optional monthly Premium subscription removes ads. Your notifications, music info and photos are processed only on your phone, only to show them on screen. The app connects to the internet only when you use the map, for the open-source map and routing services listed below.

1. Controller

StandBy is made by an independent developer. For any privacy question, contact yigit1903ask@gmail.com.

2. Data processed on your device

Notifications Only if you grant Notification access, the app name, title and text of incoming notifications are read on your phone to show them on StandBy's Dashboard. They are never sent anywhere or stored. Dismissing a notification on the Dashboard also dismisses it on your phone. You can hide content, filter by app and revoke access at any time.
Music info With the same permission, the title, artist and artwork of what is playing are shown on the music card, and play/pause/skip commands are sent to your music app.
Photos Only the photos you pick are opened on your phone for the slideshow. The app does not access the rest of your gallery.
Location Only while the map is open: to show your position, follow you while driving and track route progress. No background location, no location history.
Charging and connectivity Battery level, charger type, and whether Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and mobile data are on are read only to display them and to start StandBy automatically.
Settings and recent destinations Stored on your phone. You can clear recent destinations in Settings › Privacy.

The app does not collect contacts, call logs, messages, file lists or personal identifiers and contains no analytics or crash-reporting libraries. The advertising ID is used by Google AdMob only for the ads described in section 4.

3. Data sent over the internet (map only)

Map tiles Downloaded from OpenFreeMap. Like any web request, the server can see your IP address and the map area being viewed.
Routing When you start a route, the start and destination coordinates are sent to the free OpenStreetMap community routing server (routing.openstreetmap.de; as a fallback router.project-osrm.org). You can set your own routing server in Settings.
Destination search Your search text goes to your phone's address lookup service (Google on most phones); if nothing is found, to Photon (komoot).
Voice guidance Turn announcements are spoken by your phone's text-to-speech engine. If your engine is cloud-based (e.g. Google), the announcement text may be sent to it.

These services have their own privacy terms. StandBy keeps no copy of this data and never sends it to the developer. If you never open the map, the app does not contact these services.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

A banner ad is shown on the Settings home page. You can also choose to watch a short rewarded ad to unlock a single Premium theme, font or clock style. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may process the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. See Google Ads privacy and AdMob data disclosure.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before ads; you can change your choice in Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy options. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings › Google › Ads. Premium subscribers see no ads.

5. Subscription and payment

StandBy Premium is a monthly subscription sold through Google Play Billing. Payment happens entirely on Google Play; your card, name and billing details never reach the app or the developer. The app only checks with Google Play whether the subscription is active and stores that on your phone. Google's processing is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Cancel anytime in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

7. Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent or transfer any data to third parties. Apart from the map, routing and search services, Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play (payments) listed above, nobody receives any data.

8. Retention and deletion

All data stays on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app or choose Settings › Apps › StandBy › Storage › Clear data. Since we keep no copy, no deletion request is needed.

9. Children

StandBy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

10. Your rights

Under GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct and delete your data. Because data is kept on your device, you can exercise these rights directly on it; for any question, email the address above.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the “last updated” date above is changed.
