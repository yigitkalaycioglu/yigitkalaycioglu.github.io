Hollowfern Uploader

A small personal automation tool that publishes the daily episode of Hollowfern, an animated short-film series, to its own YouTube channel.

What it does

Once a day, the tool takes a finished video file produced by the studio, attaches its title, description and tags, and uploads it to the Hollowfern YouTube channel as a private draft for review.

Who it is for

Nobody but the channel owner. This is a single-operator tool that runs on the owner's own machine and signs in with the owner's own Google account. It is not distributed, sold, or offered to other users.

Google account access

The tool signs in with Google and requests one permission: youtube.upload. That scope allows it to upload a video file to the YouTube channel belonging to the signed-in account. Nothing else.

  • It does not read watch history, subscriptions, comments or analytics.
  • It does not modify or delete existing videos.
  • It does not access Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service.

Full details are in the privacy policy.

About the series

Hollowfern follows Fen, a young fox a long way from home. Every day, one wordless short: a single quiet moment from the journey back. Episodes are made with AI animation tools, then directed and edited by hand.
