## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a gallery painted in one cobalt — each project hangs alone on its own wall with a museum label. It refuses the dark hero, gradient name, glow, and equal card grid of the category.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched electric cobalt field (#1f3fdc family) with matte pigment grain as the main wall; navy-black (#000510) rooms for dense reading; white ink only on cobalt. Upright Bodoni display, quiet grotesk labels, no glow, no gradients on text, no tracked mono labels, no cards with borders. Works are framed images on the wall; information lives in wall labels. Light theme: walls stay cobalt, because cobalt on navy-black is the user's binding palette (PRODUCT.md, Brand Commitments) and the cobalt wall is the identity; only the rooms turn gallery-white (#f4f5f8) with navy ink.

STORY: The visitor meets the name as an exhibition title, reads a short curatorial statement, walks wall to wall through the works (three solo walls, then a salon hang), sees the studio method, reads the artist CV, and leaves at a cobalt room whose only object is the email address.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full cobalt field. Minimal nav top (YK left; Works, Process, About, Contact, TR/EN, theme right). Giant upright Bodoni "Yiğit / Kalaycıoğlu" centred, ~190px desktop. Bottom-left wall label: name (Sakarya), role, range · 13 works. Bottom-right "Get in touch →" as the primary action.

FORM: Klein-blue monochrome gallery, IMPECCABLE'S PICK (rank 1 of 7 in round two's grounded list). seed:019a4ad4f8bdf428
Second motion moment (recorded after review): the studio method is a pinned horizontal corridor on desktop, the five rooms sliding past as the visitor scrolls; it wraps into rows under reduced motion and on narrow screens.
Signature interaction: walking the gallery — works approach the viewer and their labels settle in as they reach the wall's centre, reversibly with scroll; the cobalt field's pigment nap deepens faintly under the cursor.
Motion grammar: slow, gallery-paced fades and rises (600–900ms, ease-out), no flips, blurs, particles or scattering; reduced-motion shows everything still.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
