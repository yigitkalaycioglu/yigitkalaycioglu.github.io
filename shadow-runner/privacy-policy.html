Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android game “ShadowRunner”. Last updated: 11 October 2026

In short: ShadowRunner has no accounts and we (the developer) do not run any server that receives your personal data. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob; Google and its partners process some device data (such as the advertising ID and an approximate location derived from your IP address) to deliver and measure ads. Where required by law we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy settings.

1. Who is responsible

The game is published by Yiğit (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question or request contact yeepy@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your device

Progress (levels, stars, best times, daily-puzzle streak), unlocked skins and your settings (sound, music, vibration, language, colour-blind mode) are saved in a file inside the app's private storage on your device. This file is not sent to us. The game also keeps a short list of gameplay events (for example “level started”) in memory on the device only; it is not transmitted anywhere. We have no account system and do not ask for your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos or precise location.

3. Data processed by Google AdMob and the consent tool (UMP)

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for and store your consent. These Google services may collect and process, on Google's own responsibility and in line with Google's policies:

4. Consent and how to change it

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. If you decline, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which use less data (for example to prevent fraud and limit how often an ad is shown).
  • You can review or change your choice at any time: open the game, go to Settings → Privacy settings.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Ads are never required to play. Rewarded videos are optional.

5. Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Personalised advertising and storing or reading information on your device for advertising are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) where consent is required. Showing non-personalised ads to finance the free game and preventing fraud are based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.

6. Your rights (EEA, UK, Switzerland, Turkey and elsewhere)

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Residents of Turkey have the rights listed in Article 11 of the Law on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK, No. 6698). Because we do not store your personal data on our own systems, most requests concern data held by Google; we will help you and forward requests where possible. Write to yeepy@gmail.com. See also the data deletion page.

7. Notice for California and other US states (CCPA / CPRA)

In the last 12 months the categories of personal information collected through the ad SDK are: identifiers (advertising ID, IP address), internet or other electronic network activity (ad interactions), approximate geolocation, and device information. They are used for advertising, security and fraud prevention. We do not sell personal information for money. The use of the advertising ID for cross-context behavioural advertising may count as “sharing” or a “sale” under California law. You can opt out by turning off personalised ads in your device settings (see section 4) and, where the game offers it, through Settings → Privacy settings. You also have the right to know, delete and correct information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Contact yeepy@gmail.com.

8. Children

ShadowRunner is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad requests are marked as not child-directed. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention

Data on your device stays there until you reset your progress in the game, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies (policies.google.com/technologies/retention).

10. International transfers

Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on safeguards recognised by law, for example adequacy decisions or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Security

The game stores data only in its private app storage. Communication between the ad SDK and Google uses encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the data we handle to a minimum.

12. Third-party links

The game and this website link to Google's privacy policy and to this website. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of other websites.

13. Changes to this policy

If the game starts to use data in a new way (for example a new analytics or ads service), we will update this policy and the date at the top before the change takes effect, and point out important changes in the app or in the store listing.
