Data deletion

ShadowRunner (Android). Last updated: 11 October 2026

ShadowRunner has no accounts. We (the developer) do not hold personal account data about you on any server, so there is no account to delete.

1. Delete your game data on the device

  • In the game: Settings → Reset progress (tap twice) erases progress, stars and skins.
  • Or in Android: Settings → Apps → ShadowRunner → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Uninstalling the game removes all of its data from the device.

2. Reset or delete your advertising ID

Ads in the game are provided by Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID.

  • Android 12 and later: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID).
  • Older versions: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID, and turn on Opt out of Ads Personalisation.
  • Menu names differ slightly between device makers.

You can also change your consent choice in the game at any time: Settings → Privacy settings.

3. Data held by Google

Google processes ad-related data under its own policies. You can review and delete data linked to your Google Account at myaccount.google.com and manage ad settings at myadcenter.google.com. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Ask us

If you have any question or want us to help with a deletion request, write to yeepy@gmail.com with the subject “ShadowRunner data request”. We answer within 30 days. We will never ask for your password.
