Drag the light.
Walk on shadows.

Shadow Runner is a one-finger puzzle game for Android. Your little shadow creature can only walk on shadows — move the light to stretch, shrink and swing them into bridges and ramps.

Google Play — coming soon

Learn in seconds

One finger, no text walls. An animated hand shows the idea in the first levels.

40 handcrafted levels

Forest, city and space — with second lights, moving blocks, mirrors, timed gates and coloured light.

A daily puzzle

A new puzzle every day, picked on your device. Works offline.

Made for everyone

14 languages, colour-blind friendly mode, big buttons, vibration toggle.
