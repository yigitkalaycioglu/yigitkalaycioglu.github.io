Stok+

Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android application published on Google Play as Stok+ (com.rafbarkod.plus).

Last updated: 19 September 2026  ·  Türkçe sürüm

In short

Your inventory lists stay on your phone. There is no account and no server operated by the developer. Ads and subscription payments are handled by Google; what Google collects in those two cases is described below.

1. Who is responsible

Stok+ is made by an independent developer. For any privacy question, write to yigit1903ask@gmail.com.

2. Data the app processes

Camera Used to read barcodes and labels. Frames are processed on the device by Google ML Kit, which works offline; they are never uploaded or stored. Photos you pick for barcode selection are also processed only on the device.
Microphone Enabled only while you hold the microphone button to dictate a shelf name or a quantity. The audio is passed to the speech recognition service built into Android (Google on most phones) to be turned into text; that transfer is governed by Google's privacy policy. The app keeps no recordings.
Inventory data List names, barcodes, part names, quantities and shelf/aisle/level values are kept in the app's own database on the phone. You alone decide where an exported Excel file goes.
Subscription state Whether you are subscribed, and until when a rewarded-ad export window is valid, is stored on the phone.

The app collects no location, contacts, file inventory or identity data, and contains no analytics or crash reporting libraries.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

When you export a saved list you may choose to watch a short rewarded ad. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may process your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information in order to deliver and measure ads and to prevent abuse. See Google Ads Privacy and AdMob data disclosures.

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before the ads SDK starts, where the personalised-ads choice can be changed. On any phone you can reset or delete the advertising ID under Settings › Google › Ads.

4. Subscriptions and payment

Monthly and yearly subscriptions are sold through Google Play Billing. Payment happens entirely inside Google Play; card details, your name and billing information never reach the app and are not visible to the developer. Data Google processes is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can cancel at any time under Play Store › Subscriptions.

5. Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent or transfer any data. Apart from the three services named above — Google AdMob (ads), Google Play (payment) and your phone's speech recognition service — no one receives data from the app.

6. Retention and deletion

All inventory data lives on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or choosing Settings › Apps › Stok+ › Storage › Clear data, deletes it. Since the developer holds no copy, there is no deletion request to send.

7. Children

The app is aimed at warehouse and retail staff. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR and Turkish data protection law (KVKK) you have the right to access, correct and delete your data. Because the data is kept on your own device, you can exercise these rights directly on the phone; you are still welcome to write to the address above.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above is changed. Significant changes are also noted on the store listing.
