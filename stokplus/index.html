Stok+

An Android app for counting stock shelf by shelf. Scan the product with the camera, pick or say the shelf, and share the finished count as an Excel file. Lists are kept on the phone: no account, no server.

  • Barcode reading works offline
  • No account needed
  • Lists stay on the phone
  • Kotlin
  • Jetpack Compose
  • CameraX
  • ML Kit
  • SQLite
  • Android 8.0+

Features

Everything a count needs, from reading a worn label to handing the finished list to someone else.

Reads the barcode you aim at

When several barcodes are in view, the one closest to the centre of the frame wins, so a crowded shelf doesn't give you the neighbouring product. Each read is confirmed with a beep and a vibration.

No double counts

A barcode that was just saved isn't read again while the camera is still on it; it can be counted again only after the product has left the frame.

Falls back to the label text

If a worn or torn barcode won't read, the part number is read from the printed text on the label instead.

Barcodes from a photo

Pick a photo from the gallery, see every barcode found in it and tap the right one. Handy for high shelves and labels you can't get close to.

Your own shelf layout

Define racks with up to six levels (rack, level, slot…) and a colour each. Addresses such as A-2-5 are picked from the layout, and a rack's colour follows it through the lists.

Say the shelf

“A üç”, “be zemin”, “H iki beş, on iki adet”: the shelf and the quantity are understood from speech and matched against your layout. With auto-listen on, the microphone opens by itself after each scan.

Quantities, not just lines

Enter a quantity with +/−, the keyboard or your voice. Scanning the same product into the same shelf again adds to its quantity instead of opening a duplicate line.

Batch scanning

For a shelf full of items: set the location and quantity once, and every scan after that is saved there without opening the shelf panel.

Spots split stock

During batch scanning, if the same product is already on another shelf in the list, the app says so, which catches misplaced items while you count.

Where is this part?

Search every list at once by a barcode or a fragment of a part number, and see which list and which shelf it turned up in.

Excel in and out

Share a count as an .xlsx file over WhatsApp or e-mail, or save it to the phone. The shelf layout travels the same way: open the file on another phone and its racks are imported.

Tidy lists

Long-press to select records and delete them together, with undo. Drag a record by its handle to move it; the exported file keeps the same order.

How a count goes

From an empty list to a file on someone else's desk in four steps.

  1. Start a list

    A new list is named after the date and time; rename it whenever you like.

  2. Scan

    Point the camera at the product. A beep and a vibration mean the barcode was read.

  3. Pick the shelf

    Tap or say the shelf and the quantity, and the record is saved. The shelf you used last is already selected for the next product.

  4. Export

    Share the list as Excel or save it to the phone. Each level of the shelf address gets its own column, next to barcode, quantity and date, with a total row at the end.

Saying a shelf

The app listens in Turkish and matches what it hears against the racks you defined, so only addresses that exist are accepted.

Ads and subscription

Counting, saving and searching are free. Only exporting a saved list asks for something in return.

A rewarded ad, only on export

Before an export you're offered a short rewarded ad. One ad opens exports for five minutes, so sharing a list and then saving it to the phone costs a single ad.

If no ad can be loaded, the export goes ahead anyway; you're never locked out of your own data. No ads appear while you count.

Subscription

A subscription removes the ads. It's billed through Google Play, renews automatically and can be cancelled at any time under Play Store › Subscriptions; it stays active until the end of the paid period.

Privacy

The developer runs no server, so there's nowhere for your lists to go.

PermissionWhat it's for
CameraReading barcodes and label text. Frames are processed on the phone by ML Kit and never stored or sent.
MicrophoneOnly while you dictate a shelf or a quantity. The audio goes to the phone's speech recognition service (Google on most phones); no recordings are kept.
InternetLoading rewarded ads and checking the subscription with Google Play.
VibrationConfirming a scan.

What leaves the phone

When an ad is shown, Google AdMob may process the advertising ID and device information; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland a consent form comes first. Payments happen entirely inside Google Play and never reach the app. There are no analytics or crash-reporting libraries.

Known limitations

  • Speech recognition uses the phone's own service. Without an internet connection, Google's offline Turkish voice pack has to be installed.
  • Reading part numbers from label text is tuned for common part-number formats and may not work on every label.
  • The app doesn't sync lists between phones or keep a cloud copy of them; a count is moved by exporting it.
  • The interface and voice input are in Turkish only.
